Looking Ahead

YICC is committed to empowering over 100,000 Rwandan youth to take active roles in combating climate change.

Through our programs in sensitization, adaptation, and mitigation, we are creating lasting lasting change by equipping young people with the knowledge and tools needed to lead environmental efforts.
