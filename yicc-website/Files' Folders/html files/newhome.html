Our Programs

Three comprehensive initiatives workging together to empower Rwandan youth to become climate leaders through education, innovation, and action.

SET Program

Sensitization, Engagement & Transformation

Raising awareness and transforming youth into active environment guardians through education and youth-driven activism.

CAR Program

Climate Adaptation in Rwanda

Empowering youth to develop innovative adaptation solutions for climate resilience in vulnerable communities.

CMP Program

Climate Mitigation Program

Reducing greenhouse gas emmissions through tree planting renewable energy promotion and sustainable practices.

Our Mission

To empower Rwandan high school students and youth to become environmental leaders of tomorrow, ensuring that climate solutions extend from local communities to the global stage.

Youth Empowerment

Equipping young leaders with knowledge and tools to drive climate action, through hands-on workshops and exposure to cutting-edge technology to develop home-grown environmental solutions.

Global Impact

Creating environmental products that cross borders, resonate from Rwanda to global arena.

Sustainable Future

Building resilience by utilizing and integrating blossoming technologies into environmental stewardship

Why Climate Action
Matters Now

Climate change is one of the most pressing issues affecting Rwanda and the world. The increasing frequencyv of extreme weather events, floods, landslides, droughts, and soil erosion, places an immense pressure on environment and communities - Youth and women highly vulnerable.

Young people are not just the future, they are now. With 1.8 billion youth globally, and over 60% of Rwandan population, we, this generational youth, have power, innovation, and determination to create lasting change.

The urgency of climate change demands immedaite action. We are stepping in because THERE IS NO PLANET B.
