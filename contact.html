Let's Connect

Climate change is no longer a distant threat, it is already shaping our communities, our livelihoods, and our future. Across Rwanda, young people are witnessing floods, landslides, droughts, and environmental loss firsthand. At Youth Initiative in Climate Change (YICC), we believe this reality demands more than concern, it demands action.

YICC movement brings together Rwandan students, innovators, and changemakers who are ready to protect our planet and build practical solutions rooted in our local context. From schools to communities, we equip young people with the knowledge, skills, and platforms to lead climate action through education, innovation, adaptation, and mitigation.

Whether you are a student with an idea, a volunteer eager to serve, a partner seeking impact, or a supporter ready to invest in youth-led solutions, there is a place for you here. This is not about the future alone, youth are acting now.
